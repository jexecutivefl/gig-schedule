Uber's cap: 12 hrs online driving time, then a mandatory 6 hrs fully offline before it'll let you back on — stopped-and-waiting time (like an airport lot) doesn't count against it, actively driving/queued does. Lyft runs the same structure. Nothing here pushes either limit; the 2–4pm Lyft block is there because that window is dead on Uber anyway, not to dodge a clock.

Targets are planning ranges, built from typical peak-demand windows, not guarantees — adjust the $ splits once you've got a week of your own actuals against them.

Sunday ends at 9:30pm on purpose. That's the block that makes Monday's 4am start survivable. Skipping it doesn't add a good hour — it costs you Monday morning, which is one of the best blocks of the week.
